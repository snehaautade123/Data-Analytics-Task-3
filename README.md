# Data-Analytics-Task-3
📊 Customer Revenue & Retention Performance Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes customer revenue and retention performance across regions and product categories.
The objective is to identify revenue drivers, churn risks, and regional performance trends to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed

How is revenue distributed across regions?

Which region has the highest churn risk?

Which product category drives the most revenue?

Are revenue trends stable over time?

How is revenue distributed across categories within each region?

📈 Dashboard Highlights

KPI Overview (Revenue, Customers, Churn, AOV, Purchase Frequency)

Revenue & Churn Trend Analysis

Revenue Distribution by Category

Region-wise Performance Mapping

Revenue by Region & Category Breakdown

Risk-focused Region Comparison Table

Interactive Button Slicers (Region & Category)

Executive Insights Panel

🔍 Key Insights

Revenue is evenly distributed across regions, reducing concentration risk.

Asia records the highest churn rate (~0.51), indicating retention concerns.

Europe shows relatively lower churn, reflecting better customer stability.

Electronics is the top revenue-contributing category.

Monthly revenue trend remains stable without major volatility.
